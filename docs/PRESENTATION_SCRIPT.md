# Walkback: 3-minute presentation script

Every click ("Do") and every line to say ("Say"), in order.

## Before you start (not timed)

1. Run `python -m http.server 8000`, open **http://localhost:8000** in Chrome, press **Ctrl+Shift+R**, then **F11**.
2. Click **3D**, wait for the terrain to appear, click **2D**. This makes 3D load instantly later.
3. Press **F5** to reload.
4. In the dropdown under the verdict, choose **Scenario: Quiet**.
5. Check the top right shows **CAUTION · 5 science stops · 5.8 h · 7.1 km**.

## Part 1 · 0:00–0:20 · The hook

| Do | Say |
|---|---|
| Nothing. The map with the route is on screen. | "On Mars, the most important question on a spacewalk is: *can we get home?* Help from Earth is 20 minutes away by radio, and since NASA lost MAVEN, its solar-storm monitor at Mars, crews depend on storm forecasts made from Earth. Meet Walkback." |

## Part 2 · 0:20–0:50 · The plan

| Do | Say |
|---|---|
| Point at the map. | "This is Jezero crater, where NASA's Perseverance rover is exploring." |
| Right panel: click the **Layers** tab. Tick **Photogeologic map**, wait 2 s, untick it. | "We layer real NASA data: the rock map…" |
| Tick **THEMIS thermal inertia**, wait 2 s, untick it. | "…heat data that shows soft sand…" |
| Click the **Plan** tab. Point at the blue route and numbered badges. | "…and the terrain. Walkback picks five real Perseverance sample sites and the easiest safe route between them." |
| Point at the yellow **CAUTION** box and the first check with the **BINDING** tag. | "Every check here is a real limit: slope, suit air, daylight. Verdict: CAUTION, because this walk uses almost all of the 6-hour spacewalk." |

## Part 3 · 0:50–1:25 · The storm (key moment)

| Do | Say |
|---|---|
| In the dropdown under the verdict, choose **Scenario: Warning**. | "Now NASA forecasts a solar storm hitting Mars within 10 hours. The crew must stay within 30 minutes of shelter." |
| Pause 1 second. Point at the map: the bright zone shrinks. | "Watch the safe zone shrink." |
| Point at the red **NO-GO** and the red box listing dropped stops. | "The delta stops drop out, and the verdict turns NO-GO, instantly." |

## Part 4 · 1:25–2:00 · Mid-spacewalk emergency

| Do | Say |
|---|---|
| Set the dropdown back to **Scenario: Quiet**. | "But what if the crew is already out there?" |
| Click **Go** (top bar). | "This is the astronaut's tablet view." |
| Drag the **Position along plan** slider to about **40%**, until "Next" says **On station: Wildcat Ridge**. | "They're at Wildcat Ridge, 84 minutes from the habitat." |
| Point at **To shelter now: 84 min**. | "Now imagine a storm warning comes in. They need to be within 30 minutes of shelter, and they're 84 minutes out." |
| Click the red **⚠ ABORT** button. | "One tap: Abort. Here's the route home, 1 hour 24 minutes, or 2 hours 48 if one astronaut has to help an injured partner." |

## Part 5 · 2:00–2:20 · The fix: a rover

| Do | Say |
|---|---|
| Click **Plan** (top bar). Set the dropdown to **Scenario: Warning**. | "Our solution for storm days:" |
| Right panel, **Plan** tab: scroll down to **Rover assistance** and tick **Pressurized rover parked as a second safe haven**. | "drive a pressurized rover out and walk from it. The rover is a shelter," |
| Point at **4 science stops** and the second bright zone. | "so even during the storm, the delta science comes back." |

## Part 6 · 2:20–2:40 · The wow: 3D

| Do | Say |
|---|---|
| Click **3D** (top right). | "And here's the plan in 3D, on real Mars terrain." |
| Click **🧑‍🚀 Astronaut view**. Camera dropdown: **Front camera**. Speed dropdown: **10× speed**. Click **▶ Walk route**. | "The two-person crew walks the route; at each sample site, one kneels to take a rock sample while the partner holds the sample bag." |

## Part 7 · 2:40–3:00 · Why trust it, and the close

| Do | Say |
|---|---|
| Leave the astronauts walking. Right panel: click the **Evidence** tab and scroll to **Perseverance backtest**. Point at **98.5%**. | "We tested our routes against where Perseverance actually drove: 98.5% of the ground it crossed, our model says is walkable. Every number is from NASA data or clearly marked as our assumption." |
| Look at the judges. | "Walkback: always know the way home." |

## Tips

- Practise with a timer at least 5 times. Parts 4 and 5 have the most clicks, so practise those most.
- If you fall behind, skip the Layers ticks in Part 2; just point at the map.
- Record the whole thing as a backup video before the day.
