Model assumptions

Effort: Minetti et al. 2002 walking cost (Earth treadmill data) × crew+suit mass. Mars gravity and suit stiffness are NOT modelled, so effort is a relative index, not a flight value. Pace: the flat pace you set, with a Tobler-style slope penalty (assumed). Never-exceed slope 20° follows Artemis xEVA walking limits. The 6 h EVA, 469 W average effort and shelter limit are assumptions you can change. Baseline dose uses Curiosity RAD's 0.64 mSv/day average and ignores suit shielding and solar storms.
