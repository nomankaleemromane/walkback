      Walkback

      The Mars EVA planner that always knows the way home

      On Mars, the first question on any spacewalk is can we get home? Walkback plans a Marswalk to real Perseverance sample sites in Jezero crater and checks, at every step, that the crew can still walk back to shelter before air, daylight or a solar-storm limit runs out. It layers 10 datasets from 8 NASA missions.

      1. Plan: the route, stops and GO / CAUTION / NO-GO verdict, with every safety check and its source.
      2. Go: the astronaut's tablet view during the walk: time to shelter and the margin left.
      3. Abort: one tap for the safest way home.
